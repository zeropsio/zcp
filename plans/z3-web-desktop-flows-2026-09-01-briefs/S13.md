# Slice brief: S13 — Per-ZCP branded agent authorization card

Self-contained. Product rules are in `docs/spec-z3.md` §5.4 and §8.2 and
`../z3/docs/internals/zerops/design-system.md` §§1–4.

**Outcome**: The current ZCP service panel presents its real Claude Code/Codex snapshot as a clear,
branded and responsive authorization flow on both web and desktop.

**Allowed scope**
- Files: `apps/web/src/components/zerops/ZeropsAgentAuthCard.tsx`;
  `apps/web/src/components/zerops/ZeropsAgentAuthCard.test.tsx`.

§8.2 (server-driven phases/actions); design system §§1–4 (atoms, language, status semantics).

- `keeps Claude browser authorization free of a device-code action` — layer: unit.
