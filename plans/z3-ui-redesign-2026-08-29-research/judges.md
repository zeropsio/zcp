### Verdicts
- **skin-first**: Safest, most executable of the four. Verified: BranchToolbar.logic.ts:74 literally returns "Local checkout" as claimed; ZeropsLifecycleStrip mounts at ChatView.tsx:6922 (cited 6906-6928); index.css's html[data-theme-id] remap starts at line 1552 exactly as cited. Its own 'seams' section is honest that the map stays a right-panel tab and cards stay inside the 'Used N tools' fold by default — the two things a Zerops user would most want surfaced are exactly what's deferred, which caps journeyQuality despite excellent token/type/status fidelity.
- **project-first-ia**: Highest-fidelity transplant of the actual GUI page (org pill, project cards with FULL ACCESS/EU CENTRAL chips, mint panel) — verified Sidebar.tsx is exactly 3,960 lines as cited and grep for 'also works on' in apps/web/src/zerops returns zero matches, confirming its own honest gap-disclosure. But it rebuilds Sidebar.tsx/thread navigation from scratch and explicitly demotes cross-project thread browsing (its own Risk #4) — a real regression for an agentic tool whose daily use is juggling many parallel threads across projects, not one project at a time. Feasibility is the weakest of the four by its own admission (DnD reorder deferred to 'v2', L-effort phases).
- **agent-console**: Best single ergonomic idea (docked question/credential cards at the composer) and the most honest treatment of the not-yet-built credential verb (renders only a GUI deep-link until it exists, never fabricates a chat-paste flow). But verified the math behind its own thesis undercuts it: THREAD_MAIN_CONTENT_MIN_WIDTH is 640px (threadSidebarWidth.ts:4, confirmed via 40*16) and sidebar is 256px, so 256+640+280(rail) already leaves no room for any right-panel surface at 1440px, and the proposal's own Risk #1 concedes the 'always in view' rail collapses to a 44px dot column at the most common laptop width (1280-1439) — the signature feature fails in the common case by the proposal's own arithmetic. It also drops the rail entirely on mobile, so the thesis has zero cross-client presence there.
- **system-first**: Strongest governance and cross-client engineering of the four. Verified with unusual precision: total Tailwind palette-utility occurrences across apps/web/src (excluding tests) is exactly 217 as claimed, and Sidebar.tsx has exactly 34 such occurrences once ring/fill/stroke/etc. prefixes are included, matching the proposal's hotspot table. RIGHT_PANEL_KINDS, THEME_COLOR_ROLES (57), and the 981px inline-panel threshold are all confirmed byte-for-byte. Its weakness: it proposes a brand-new packages/brand workspace package as a second shared source of Zerops-ness alongside ZEROPS_THEME in packages/shared — more ceremony than the other three achieve for the same fixed-status-tone need with zero new packages — and, like skin-first, sequences the single-bundle auth-gate fix (the actual first thing a new user hits today per __root.tsx's requires-auth branch) into its second-to-last phase rather than earlier, so journeyQuality on day one lags its own system rigor.

### Grafts
- [agent-console] Dock the question card and (future) credential card at the composer rather than only inline in the timeline, and explicitly render only a 'set it in the Zerops GUI' fallback link for the credential card until the zcp storage verb actually exists on the server. — _It is the only proposal that refuses to design a fake mechanism for a feature the backend doesn't have yet (the credential verb is owner-designed, per brief §8.3) — the honest fallback prevents a design that would otherwise imply chat-paste secrets, which the brief explicitly forbids._
- [project-first-ia] The literal GUI-parity picker and project-card visuals: org pill + project cards carrying FULL ACCESS / EU CENTRAL (PRG1) chips, a thread-count summary line, and a dashed '+ New project' card matching the GUI's __project-add-button idiom. — _Highest continuityWithGui of any single surface across all four proposals, verified against the shared screenshots (03-after-login.png, 05-project-detail.png) — it is the one place a Zerops GUI user would recognize the exact page shape, not just the color grammar, and can be grafted into any winner's landing/picker without inheriting the sidebar rewrite that makes the rest of that proposal risky._
- [system-first] Drift guards as executable tests, not review habits: byte-equality generator check (mirroring the real generate-uniwind-themes.test.ts pattern), role-coverage + contrast assertions on the new theme, and a no-literal-color lint on Zerops-owned directories with a concrete file-level burn-down list. — _This is the only proposal that ports the fork's own proven pattern (icons:check, generate-uniwind-themes --check) to the new theme work, turning 'stay on-brand' into a CI failure instead of a PR-review ask — directly answers the stated long-term risk of three shells drifting under agent maintenance._
- [skin-first] Sequence phases 0-3 (theme registration, default look, primitives, brand sweep) to touch zero files under apps/web/src/zerops/**, saving the actual Zerops-surface restyle for one last phase built entirely from primitives that already changed. — _This is the most realistic migration ordering of the four for a codebase where other streams (S4 auth-gate, S6 product surfaces) are actively editing exactly those zerops/** files — it minimizes merge collision risk without requiring the other three proposals' larger structural bets._

### Fatal flaws
- [agent-console] The rail's own headline promise ('project always in view') fails at the most common laptop width by the proposal's own math. — _agent-console: sidebar 256px + thread min 640px (verified: apps/web/src/components/threadSidebarWidth.ts:4, THREAD_MAIN_CONTENT_MIN_WIDTH = 40 * 16 = 640) + rail 280px already sums to 1176px before any right-panel surface (diff/files, min ~320-448px per rightPanelLayout.ts's w-[min(42vw,28rem)]) is opened. The proposal's own Risk #1 concedes the rail collapses to a 44px dot-column-with-tooltips fallback at 1280-1439px — i.e. on a standard 13" laptop with a diff panel open, which it names as the common case._
- [project-first-ia] Cross-project thread browsing — the primary daily surface of a multi-project agentic coding tool — is demoted to a per-project ThreadsCard rebuilt against Sidebar.tsx's resolvers, dropping drag-and-drop pinned reorder to a deferred 'v2'. — _Verified apps/web/src/components/Sidebar.tsx is exactly 3,960 lines as the proposal cites, confirming the scale of what it is replacing. The proposal's own Risk #1 ('The sidebar rewrite loses features silently') and Risk #4 ('The angle demotes threads') admit the trade rather than hide it, but for a tool whose unit of work is a thread and whose users routinely run many threads across several projects in parallel, losing the always-visible cross-project fleet view is a workflow regression, not a cosmetic one._
- [skin-first] The two surfaces a Zerops user most wants foregrounded — service status and the agent's Zerops-tool activity — are the two things left exactly where T3 already buries them: the map stays a right-panel tab opened by a keystroke, and Zerops cards stay physically inside the collapsed 'Used N tools' fold unless a later, explicitly out-of-scope S6 change escapes them. — _Confirmed via the proposal's own 'What stays T3-shaped, deliberately' section (map is 'one Z away, not the page'; cards render inside the fold, 'a MessagesTimeline change and an S6 product call') — on day one, opening a fresh Zerops Code thread looks and behaves almost identically to opening a fresh T3 thread, which cuts directly against the brief's 'looks and feels like Zerops' bar for the first-impression journey the task asks to judge._
- [system-first] Introduces a brand-new shared workspace package (packages/brand) as a second source of 'the Zerops look' alongside the ZEROPS_THEME roles it also adds to the existing packages/shared/src/themePalettes.ts — more infrastructure ceremony than any of the other three proposals need for the identical technical requirement (fixed status tones independent of the active theme), which the other three solve with a CSS/TS block inside the existing zerops component directory with zero new packages. — _system-first's own cross-client system section lists packages/shared/src/themePalettes.ts (ZEROPS_THEME) and packages/brand/ (mark paths, fixed status tones, icon map, copy glossary) as two separate homes for brand truth, while its thesis is explicitly 'one source' — the other three proposals (skin-first's '--zerops-* block', agent-console's 'kept local to apps/web/src/components/zerops/', project-first-ia folding it into the theme table itself) reach the same outcome without a new package boundary or its added workspace/build/zone-classification overhead._

### Verdicts
- **skin-first — Recoat**: Verified accurate on its citations (ZeropsLifecycleStrip at ChatView.tsx:6922, ZeropsToolCard mount at MessagesTimeline.tsx:2605, ZeropsServiceMap.tsx:67 rounded-xl/border-border/55/bg-card/20 all confirmed byte-exact). Safest, most shippable plan — phases 0-3 genuinely touch no zerops/** files, so it will not collide with S4/S6. But it is honest to a fault about its own ceiling: it explicitly leaves 'a thread inbox with a hero composer' and puts the mint zcp panel behind a right-panel toggle a user must remember to open, so the single strongest Zerops signal is invisible on the default screen. Weakest answer to the judge's central question ('would a Zerops user recognise it instantly').
- **project-first-ia — Controls & Content**: The most literal, pixel-for-grammar transplant of the actual GUI screen (org pill, cards column, chips, mint panel at the foot) — verified against 05-project-detail-full.png, it is the closest visual match of the four. Citations check out exactly (AppSidebarLayout.tsx:227-233's isOnSettings/legacySidebarEnabled ternary confirmed at lines 229-236; Sidebar.tsx confirmed 3960 lines). But its own Risk #1 is real and verified: replacing T3's thread-navigation primitive with a project-cards pane demotes the single most-used surface (a coding-agent user's cross-project thread list) to one card among several, and the DnD-reorder/prewarm feature set has no stated recovery path before v2. Highest brand payoff, highest engineering and daily-workflow risk of the four.
- **agent-console — Rail**: Keeps the thread as primary (T3's actual daily-use surface) while adding a compressed, always-visible GUI-grammar rail — a good compromise between skin-first's invisibility and project-first-ia's demotion of threads. Its own width math is verified against the real constants (THREAD_MAIN_CONTENT_MIN_WIDTH = 640px, THREAD_SIDEBAR default 256px confirmed in threadSidebarWidth.ts) and the self-flagged Risk #1 is genuine: sidebar(256)+thread(640)+rail(280)+a panel(400)=1576 exceeds even a 1440px laptop, so the signature 'project always in view' idea degrades to a tooltip-only dot column on the most common developer display whenever a diff/terminal panel is also open. Its explicit refusal to port the rail to mobile ('a lie about the device') is the most mature platform judgment of the four but costs it cross-client shape coherence — the phone gets a genuinely different interface, not a smaller version of the same one.
- **system-first — One Map, Three Shells**: Wins on rigor: THEME_COLOR_ROLES (verified exactly 57 roles at themePalettes.ts:18-77) plus a new packages/brand fixed-token layer, a root generator with --check in CI, byte-equality/contrast/no-literal-color/vocabulary drift tests, and a visual-regression harness across web+mobile+desktop is the most complete answer to 'one coherent system' and to the long-run drift risk that is the real threat once agents maintain three shells. But structurally it keeps T3's shell almost exactly like skin-first (map stays an on-demand right-panel surface, no persistent GUI-shaped anchor), so relative to its very large infrastructure investment (a brand-new workspace package, three-shell generator, three-shell screenshot harness — all before P4 touches a single Zerops-branded surface) its day-one 'instantly Zerops' payoff is no bigger than skin-first's cheaper plan. The most defensible plan to live with for years; not the boldest answer to the brief's 'recognise it instantly' test.

### Grafts
- [project-first-ia] Transplant the GUI's own app-bar (teal-tinted mark pill + org/project chevron menu + ⌘K pill) into the sidebar header, and reuse the picker's exact card anatomy — status dot, name at 20/500, FULL ACCESS / EU CENTRAL chips, a dashed '+ New project' card — verbatim from 03-after-login.png / 05-project-detail-full.png. — _Verified as the closest pixel-for-grammar match to the real GUI of anything across the four proposals; it is cheap (touches only the sidebar header and the picker, not Sidebar.tsx's 3960-line navigation core) and gives every proposal's weakest area — day-one instant recognition — its biggest single win without inheriting project-first-ia's thread-demotion risk._
- [agent-console] The full-width lifecycle 'band' under the header (not a small pill) as the compact reading of the strip, with milestone cards (plan/import/deploy/verify/error) explicitly escaping the 'Used N tools' fold while mount/subdomain stay folded. — _Gives the lifecycle state far more visual weight than a header pill without needing a new persistent column, and makes an explicit, testable product call on the fold-escape question that verified.md leaves open — worth adopting regardless of which IA angle wins._
- [agent-console] On mobile, do not shrink the desktop pattern: replace the persistent Zerops surface with a thread-row/header subtitle (the strip phrase) plus a pull-up sheet for the map, and treat the Live Activity/Dynamic Island payload as a first-class, mobile-only expression of the same lifecycle string. — _The most credible 'Zerops mobile language' argument of the four — it explicitly derives a phone-native shape from the shared tokens/copy instead of transplanting a desktop layout, which is exactly the test the brief poses ('a credible Zerops mobile language rather than T3 with new colours')._
- [skin-first] The migration-ordering discipline: sequence tokens/primitives/vocabulary (phases that touch no zerops/** files) strictly before any change to the live Zerops surfaces the S4/S6 streams are actively editing (ZeropsProjectPicker, ZeropsServiceMap, ZeropsLifecycleStrip, ZeropsToolCard). — _The only proposal that explicitly reasons about concurrent-stream collision with evidence (naming the exact components S4/S6 touch today) — this ordering rule should govern whichever angle is built, including system-first's own P4._

### Fatal flaws
- [agent-console — Rail] The signature 'project always in view' rail does not fit alongside a right panel on the most common developer laptop viewport. — _Verified constants: apps/web/src/components/threadSidebarWidth.ts sets THREAD_SIDEBAR default 256px and THREAD_MAIN_CONTENT_MIN_WIDTH = 640px. 256 (sidebar) + 640 (thread min) + 280 (rail) + ~400-448px (right panel, per RIGHT_PANEL_SHEET_CLASS_NAME's 28rem max) = 1576-1624px, which exceeds a 1440px MacBook logical resolution — exactly the failure mode the proposal's own Risk #1 admits ('a column of dots with tooltips')._
- [project-first-ia — Controls & Content] Replaces T3's actual highest-frequency surface (the cross-project thread list, Sidebar.tsx, verified 3960 lines with virtualisation, dnd-kit pinned reorder, snooze shelves, prewarm) with a project-scoped Threads card, deferring DnD reorder to v2 with no interim substitute named. — _apps/web/src/components/Sidebar.tsx = 3960 lines (wc -l verified). The proposal's own Risk #1 names 'search, multi-select, dnd-kit pinned reorder, snooze shelves, jump hints, prewarm — none exported as a library' and ships v1 as 'a declared subset.' For a coding-agent client whose primary daily action is switching between concurrent agent threads, shipping a known feature regression to gain GUI-card fidelity is a real cost to the core workflow, not a cosmetic one._
- [skin-first — Recoat] The one component that carries the most Zerops brand weight (the mint zcp panel with the AUTHORIZED tray) sits inside a right-panel surface that is closed by default in ordinary use. — _Confirmed RIGHT_PANEL_KINDS in apps/web/src/rightPanelStore.ts includes 'zerops' as one of eight kinds opened via the add-menu / Z shortcut, not mounted by default; the proposal's own 'Right panel — Services' section states the panel opens only on a shortcut or an optional non-committal 'default-open' mitigation ('Recommended one-line default'). Screenshot 05-project-detail-full.png shows this exact panel is the FIRST thing a Zerops user sees on a project page today — hiding it behind a keystroke directly undercuts the 'recognise it instantly' bar the brief sets._
- [system-first — One Map, Three Shells] Front-loads a disproportionate share of new infrastructure (a brand-new packages/brand workspace, a three-shell token generator, byte-equality/contrast/lint test suites, a new web+desktop visual-regression harness) before any Zerops-branded surface itself changes shape, so the visible 'looks like Zerops' payoff arrives no earlier, and is no larger day-one, than skin-first's far cheaper plan. — _The proposal's own migration table places 'P4 — Zerops surfaces in Zerops idiom' (map, cards, credential card, landing/picker) after P0 (words/marks), P1 (theme default), P2 (generator + packages/brand + delete hand copies), P3 (primitives); P2 alone requires a wholly new workspace package plus CI wiring for an icons:check-equivalent — real net-new scope with no direct visual payoff of its own._

### Verdicts
- **skin-first**: Lowest structural risk of the four and the most disciplined about not colliding with the live S4/S6 streams (phases 0-3 verifiably touch no zerops/** file). Every code citation checked out (Sidebar.tsx 3960 lines, ZeropsPanel.tsx exactly 58 lines, ZeropsServiceMap.tsx:67 row class, DesktopWindow.ts:34-35/128-130, ChatView.tsx:6922/7180). Its own risk #1 is honest and correct: it stays a thread-inbox skin, not a redesign — the weakest brandFidelity/continuity claim of the four by its own admission.
- **project-first-ia**: The most literal, most convincing reproduction of the actual Zerops project-detail page a user already knows, and the best-researched of the four (it is the only proposal that correctly distinguishes what already exists in packages/client-runtime/src/zerops — verified: api.ts, newProject.ts, registration.ts, session.ts are there today — from what still needs promoting). But it bets the whole redesign on retiring Sidebar.tsx's product path and deleting ZeropsPanel.tsx / the `zerops` right-panel kind, both files S6 is actively extending per the fork's own stream map — the single biggest feasibility liability of the four proposals, and self-disclosed only partially (labelled 'S4/S6-coupled' for cards, not for the pane rebuild itself).
- **agent-console**: The boldest single idea (a project state that never scrolls away) and the most honest about its own math, but the honesty is itself the problem: its own risk #1 computes sidebar 256 + thread 640 + rail 280 + panel 400 and concedes it does not fit even a 1440px window with a diff open, which undercuts the thesis's headline claim ('project always in view') for the laptop sizes most users actually have. Mobile explicitly opts out of the rail metaphor entirely, so 'one thesis, three shells' is really 'one thesis on web, two workarounds elsewhere' — the weakest crossClientCoherence of the four.
- **system-first**: Winner. Keeps T3's proven, load-bearing shell (verified: the sidebar's existing `isOnSettings ? SettingsSidebarNav : legacySidebarEnabled ? LegacyThreadSidebar : ThreadSidebar` branch in AppSidebarLayout.tsx is real and cheap to extend a fourth way) so it carries the least structural risk while still resolving the mobile-with-zero-Zerops-code problem (verified: apps/mobile/src greps for 'zerops' return nothing) with a real, spec-backed vocabulary layer instead of ad hoc per-screen work. Its drift guards (packages/brand separation, DS-1..DS-6, a concrete literal-colour lint with real current counts) are the closest fit to this house's own testing culture (zcp's atoms_lint / TestNoBareZeropsURIInAgentContent pattern). Its own generator ambition is understated: the only existing precedent (generate-uniwind-themes.mts, verified 262 lines, one target) emits a single CSS variant file, while system-first's P2 generator must emit six heterogeneous targets (web CSS, mobile CSS, boot HTML, brandColors.ts, manifest, icon exports) before P3 can even start — that is realistically an L phase mislabelled M.

### Grafts
- [project-first-ia] Replace the right panel's fixed 980px media-query breakpoint (verified real: RIGHT_PANEL_INLINE_LAYOUT_MEDIA_QUERY = "(max-width: 980px)" in rightPanelLayout.ts) with a container query on the main content column, following the precedent ChatHeader.tsx already sets with @container/header-actions and @3xl/header-actions (verified at lines 293/384). — _It is the one piece of the pane/rail proposals' width-budget problem that is both genuinely clever and cheap — it lets whatever holds the service map degrade gracefully when a wide left surface and a right panel compete for space, without inventing new breakpoint plumbing. Grafts cleanly onto system-first's existing right-panel `zerops` kind._
- [project-first-ia] The 'focus mode' pattern — a card in a persistent list expands to fill the available column when opened — as the mitigation for feature parity with Sidebar.tsx's pinned/snoozed/dnd-reorder functionality (verified real and large: 3960 lines). — _It is an honest way to keep a lighter-weight card list without silently dropping T3's real thread-management features, and it generalizes to any place system-first or skin-first later wants a denser list inside a lighter chrome (e.g. an expanded services group)._
- [agent-console] Route the Live Activity / Dynamic Island payload directly off the shared strip-phrase function (zeropsStripState / strip.ts, verified real and tested via strip.test.ts) rather than building any bespoke mobile-only summarization. — _It is free given the feed that already exists, ties precisely to the drift-guard principle every proposal endorses (one phrase, one source), and is the single mobile feature that has no desktop equivalent — worth calling out explicitly rather than leaving implicit._
- [agent-console] The three-state responsive fallback for a persistent state surface (full column → icon/dot column with tooltips → panel-on-demand) as an explicit, tested degradation ladder rather than a single breakpoint. — _Even though it does not rescue agent-console's own width math, the pattern itself is sound and reusable: it gives any 'always visible' Zerops surface (system-first's or skin-first's map) a named, deliberate behavior at 1280-1439px instead of an ad hoc squeeze._
- [skin-first] The explicit staging rule 'phases 0-3 touch no zerops/** file; the Zerops-surface polish is the last, isolated phase' as a literal PR-review gate, not just a planning note. — _Verified consequential: the fork's own stream map (S4 owns the auth-gate branch in __root.tsx/hostedPairing.ts, S6 owns ZeropsPanel.tsx/ZeropsServiceMap.tsx/ZeropsToolCard.tsx) means any design work landing before those files stabilize is destined to conflict. Every proposal should adopt this rule regardless of which IA wins._

### Fatal flaws
- [project-first-ia] Phase 2 deletes ZeropsPanel.tsx and the `zerops` right-panel kind and rebuilds the service map as a new standing pane, while these are exactly the files the fork's own S6 stream is actively extending (the credential-card verb, Data Console wiring). The proposal labels only its Phase 4 cards work 'S4/S6-coupled' — the pane rebuild that removes S6's working files in Phase 2 is not gated the same way. — _Verified in the z3 repo: apps/web/src/components/zerops/ZeropsPanel.tsx is exactly 58 lines (real, small, and live); apps/web/src/rightPanelStore.ts's RIGHT_PANEL_KINDS array literally includes "zerops" among diff/files/file/preview/terminal/pull-request/agents. zcp's CLAUDE.local.md documents S6 as an active, owner-tracked stream on this exact surface._
- [agent-console] The proposal's own arithmetic in Risk #1 shows the four-region layout (sidebar 256 + thread min 640 + rail 280 + a 400px right panel) does not fit inside 1440px, and the text concedes it barely fits 1440 only with the rail already collapsed to a 44px dot column when a diff panel is open on a 13-inch laptop. — _The proposal's own Risk #1 section states the math and the fallback explicitly; apps/web/src/components/threadSidebarWidth.ts's sidebar width (256/208 min) and the THREAD_MAIN_CONTENT_MIN_WIDTH constant it cites are real, verified layout constants already governing the existing sidebar-resize logic in AppSidebarLayout.tsx._
