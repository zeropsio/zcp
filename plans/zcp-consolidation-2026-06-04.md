## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Six product-side roots (R1, R2, R3, R4, R5, R6, R7) all instantiate ONE conceptual defect with two faces of the same coin: ZCP either (a) re-derives a fact at the point of use from a weaker proxy instead of recording it once from the authority that owns it (single-owner violation), or (b) presents/persists/gates on its own stored copy of a fact — the full validation set, a hand-authored value, a stale PID-keyed session, a never-recorded capability, an undelivered-but-composed bundle, a discarded diagnosis — instead of deriving fresh from the live owner and informing the agent (presentation/inform violation). Every recurring eval friction traces to one of these two: a TELL that drifted from its CHECK because they were authored separately, or a payload that obstructs/misinforms the agent because ZCP trusted its stored intent over live truth. The fix family is uniform and SUBTRACTIVE in spirit: introduce or extend exactly ONE owner per concept (a relevance+budget composer for R1, OwnerBindings at the render seam for R2, RecipeShape-derives-plan for R3, ServesHTTP-stamped-at-provision for R4, project-scoped session resolver for R5, a complete-corrective-emitting gate for R6, a live-scaling snapshot projection for R7) and make every reader derive from it — which DELETES hand-gating axes, 2-role guards, re-asserted constants, and stale stamps rather than adding machinery.

## UNIFYING PATTERN
The single deepest pattern is the Information-Contract "one-owner-per-concept + the-tell-derives-from-the-check + source-of-truth-is-the-live-owner" triad, violated in two recurring modes. MODE A (drift): a fact has an authoritative owner (schema, handler, live platform, import-YAML, the classifier's own verdict, the recipe YAML) but ZCP re-authors or re-derives it at the point of use — bare nodejs@22 vs live composite, atom claims vs schema-required, plan vs recipe YAML, ServesHTTP vs raw type-string, diagnosedFailureClass discarded then asked-for, prod constants vs live scaling. MODE B (obstruct/misinform): ZCP trusts its own stored/computed copy over the live owner and either dumps it whole (full topology / all-routes / full ImportYAML on a decision made on fit alone), withholds it (export bundle composed but gated behind git-push), persists it stale (PID-keyed session dropped on reconnect, append-only REFLOG advertising gone infra), or frames a healthy state as failure (enabled:false, deployed:true-on-502, generic "go read logs" refusal). The already-shipped deploy-gate deletion is the canonical exemplar of fixing MODE B — it proved that a hard gate on a safe action is the wrong shape and that the right move is to INFORM with the live truth the response already carries. Every root in this plan generalizes that exemplar: replace "ZCP's stored copy obstructs/dumps" with "the single owner informs, ranked and derived."

**Architectural root cause:** The pipeline models APPLICABILITY (does this atom match the envelope? boolean) but has no model of RELEVANCE-AT-A-BUDGET (given everything competing for this payload and the agent's actual next decision, what is the curated subset and at what depth?). (1) RENDER DEPTH replaces the binary reference:bool.
