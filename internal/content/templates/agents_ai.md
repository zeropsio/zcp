**AI in the app.** When the person asks for an AI feature in their app — summaries, chat, classification, extraction, an agent — build it on the organization's AI key, never a key of your own or one asked of them: `zerops_env action="ai-key"` puts it in the Shared vault as the sensitive `NEBIUS_API_KEY`, with `NEBIUS_BASE_URL` beside it, without you ever seeing it. Have each service that calls the model read them under other names in its `run.envVariables` — `OPENAI_API_KEY: ${NEBIUS_API_KEY}` and `OPENAI_BASE_URL: ${NEBIUS_BASE_URL}` — and deploy. Nebius Token Factory is OpenAI-compatible: any OpenAI SDK reads those two variables by itself, or takes them as its `apiKey` and `baseURL`. Pick the model from Token Factory's own list, `GET $OPENAI_BASE_URL/models` run inside the service, not from memory, and keep its id in one place in the code. Never print, log or commit the key, nor echo it to check it: name it by its variable. When the organization has no key yet, say so in one sentence: an org Owner or Admin sets it in Mate under Settings › Zerops › HQ.
