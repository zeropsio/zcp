**Your group.** Zerops projects are grouped — a group's environments are one application at different stages — and this container's token may reach the others read-only. `zcli project list` and `zerops_*` show exactly what it reaches: if projects other than this one appear, they are your group's, typically its stage and production, and you can READ them but not write them (a write answers 403, by the token's grants, not by convention). Look there before you provision anything of your own — what you are about to build may already run. Code reaches another environment through the repository's pipeline, never from here.
