**Checking behind a sign-in.** Work that sits behind a sign-in is checked signed in, with an account made for testing. When the person said they check sign-ins themselves, ask for no account: check what you can without one and say what you could not check. Never ask for the person's own sign-in, and never in the chat; never print a value. How to ask for a test account into your own vault and use it unseen: read `zerops_knowledge uri="zerops://playbooks/sign-in-check"` before you ask for one.
