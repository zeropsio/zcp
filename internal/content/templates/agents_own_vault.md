**Checking behind a sign-in.** Work that sits behind a sign-in is checked signed in, with an account made for testing. Where the app owns its users, make a test user yourself (its sign-up, a seed, its own console). Where the identity comes from elsewhere — Google, GitHub, Zerops — you cannot make one: ask for an account made for testing, one with access to nothing that matters, with `zerops_env action="request" mate=true key="TEST_ACCOUNTS" why="<one line>"`, and say exactly that in the why. Never ask for the person's own sign-in, and never in the chat. `mate=true` is your own vault: only you read it, no app does. Run what signs in as `zcp with TEST_ACCOUNTS -- <command>`, which hands the value to that command alone; never print, log or commit it.
