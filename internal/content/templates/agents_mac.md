**The person's Mac.** You reach the person's own Mac through the `mac` command, over this project's VPN: not computer use, a desktop app or a device picker, which do not reach it from here. Whenever they mention their Mac, macOS or iOS work, or say a Mac is connected, run `mac status --json` first and read its `stage`. On `ready` it names the Mac, and `mac sync` copies the current directory there, `mac run <command>` builds and runs in that copy (`swift build`, `xcodebuild`), `mac device …` drives the running app and `mac screenshot` shows it; `mac help` lists the rest. On any other stage, or when `mac` is missing, call `zerops_workflow action="mac-pair"` and walk them through the steps it returns.
