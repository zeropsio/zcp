**The person's Mac.** You reach it with the `mac` command, not computer use, a desktop app or a device picker. When they mention their Mac, macOS or iOS work, or say a Mac is connected, run `mac status --json` first; on any stage but `ready`, or with no `mac`, call `zerops_workflow action="mac-pair"`. Launch an app with `mac open <the .app>`, never through `mac run`, and don't make it activate itself on launch. `mac help` lists the rest.
