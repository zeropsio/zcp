After bootstrap or adopt provision closes, service code SSHFS-mounts at `/var/www/{hostname}/` — the mount IS the service's runtime filesystem; edit with Read/Edit/Write, not SSH. `zerops.yaml` at `/var/www/{hostname}/zerops.yaml`; per-service rules MAY exist at `/var/www/{hostname}/CLAUDE.md` — read if present.
